How to use

This dashboard turns an AbuseIPDB scan (the abuseipdb-results.csv file) into clear charts and insights. Everything runs in your browser – your data never leaves the computer.

🚀 Get started

1Run a scan so you have abuseipdb-results.csv.
2Drag the CSV onto the dashed box, or click Load CSV (top-right).
3Charts, insights and the table appear instantly.
No results yet? Drag the included sample-results.csv to preview with demo data.

🎯 Reading the score (0–100)

0 Clean – no abuse reports
1-24 Low / background noise
25-74 Suspicious – look closer
75-100 Malicious – block / investigate

🧠 Smart features

  • Verdict banner – an instant plain-language summary of the whole scan.
  • Smart insights – auto-generated highlights (worst IP, top ISP, % clean).
  • Filter chips – one click to show All / Reported / Suspicious / Malicious.
  • Click a stat card or pie slice – instantly filters the table to that group.
  • Click an IP – copies it to your clipboard.

🔎 Search, filter & sort

  • Search any IP, ISP, or country code.
  • Country menu narrows to one country.
  • Click any column header to sort (again to reverse). Sort by Score to see the worst first.

💾 Export

  • Apply a filter (e.g. Suspicious), then Export saves just those rows – ready for a report or blocklist.
1-click workflow: Load CSV → click the Suspicious chip → sort by Score → review red/orange → Export.
